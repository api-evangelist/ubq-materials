# UBQ Materials

> UBQ Materials is a climate-tech company founded in 2012 that converts unsorted mixed household waste — including organics and hard-to-recycle plastics — into UBQ, a climate-positive thermoplastic that substitutes for oil-based plastics in manufacturing. A Certified B Corporation backed by Battery Ventures, with a full-scale production facility in Bergen op Zoom, Netherlands (80,000 tons annual capacity) and an R&D center in Tze'elim, Israel. UBQ Materials publishes no public API or developer documentation.
